Plugins by tfensign for MGMT 803.

## Penguoom

A Doom-style FPS starring a penguin, fighting through 10 winter sectors (including a Christmas Village) against penguins, polar bears, walruses, and stoats.

**▶ Play in your browser:** https://tfensign.github.io/Penguoom/

Works with keyboard/mouse or touch. Source: [`Penguoom/index.html`](Penguoom/index.html) — a single self-contained HTML file (canvas raycaster, Web Audio sound/music, no build step). Full source and history at [github.com/tfensign/Penguoom](https://github.com/tfensign/Penguoom).
