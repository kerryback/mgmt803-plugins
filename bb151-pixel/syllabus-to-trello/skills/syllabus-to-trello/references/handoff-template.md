# Handoff template

Write one handoff per course, after the cards and markers are created and verified. Keep it short enough to read in two minutes. A future session should be able to pick up from it alone.

```markdown
# <Course code> — Trello and Calendar Handoff
**<Course name> | Status as of <date>**

## Course schedule
<How many live sessions, which calendar they're on, weekday and time with time zone, and the dates.>

## Trello setup
<The list name and board, how many cards and what kinds (weekly async, quizzes, prep, group, exam). State that due dates are set to *personal targets*. Say plainly what couldn't be set, such as the reminder field or card membership, and what that means: "Trello advance notifications are not confirmed.">

## Calendar setup
<How many markers, on which calendar, at what time, with which popup reminders, and whether they were verified. State that the markers are planning targets, not work sessions or official deadlines. Mention anything deliberately not duplicated, such as existing class events.>

| Date, <marker time and zone> | Personal target |
|---|---|
| <Day, Mon D> | <Card name> — **tentative** where it applies |

<One or two lines on sequencing: when assignments release, what must be done before what, and which windows are short.>

## Dates to confirm on the course site
- **<Item>:** <why it's uncertain, and what depends on it>

When any official date changes, update **both** its Trello card and its calendar marker. Keep the official deadline distinct from the personal target.

## Academic-integrity notes
<Where AI is allowed (such as studying concepts) and where it isn't (such as quizzes, exams or note-sheet text), plus any exam-aid rules like a one-page note sheet.>
```
