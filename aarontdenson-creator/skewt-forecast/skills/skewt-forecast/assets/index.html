Why temperature & dewpoint matter

Temperature helps pilots assess air density and aircraft performance. Dewpoint describes moisture in the air. When temperature approaches dewpoint, air is near saturation, so cloud or fog formation becomes more likely and visibility may fall. The temperature–dewpoint spread is also used for a rough cloud-base estimate.
