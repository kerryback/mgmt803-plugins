    Reading the diagram

    • Nu -- the nucleophile, attacking the electrophilic carbon.
    • LG -- the leaving group, departing with the bonding electrons.
    • Solid bond = fully formed. Dashed bond = partially formed/broken.
    • In SN2 the three other groups flip through a flat transition state, like an umbrella turning inside out -- this is the inversion of configuration.
    • In SN1 the leaving group departs first, leaving a flat carbocation that the nucleophile can then attack from either face.
